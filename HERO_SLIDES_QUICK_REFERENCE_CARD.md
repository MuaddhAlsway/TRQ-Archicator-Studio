## 🎥 ADD VIDEOS (English Slides)

1. Click "Hero Slides (EN)"
2. Click "Edit" on a slide
3. Scroll to "Video Management (Up to 3 videos)"
4. For each video:
   - Paste URL in "Video URL" field
   - Add text in "Video Text" field (optional)
5. Click "Save Slide"

**Video Format:**
- MP4 format
- 1920x1080 resolution
- 30-60 seconds duration

---

## 🌍 ADD ARABIC (Arabic Slides)
