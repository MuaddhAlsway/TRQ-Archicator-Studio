## 🎬 HOW TO ADD VIDEOS

### In Admin Panel

1. **Click "Hero Slides (EN)"**
2. **Click "Edit" on a slide**
3. **Scroll to "Video Management (Up to 3 videos)"**
4. **For each video:**
   - Paste video URL in "Video URL" field
   - Add optional text in "Video Text" field
   - Click "Save Slide"

### Video Requirements

- **Format:** MP4
- **Codec:** H.264 video, AAC audio
- **Resolution:** 1920x1080 recommended
- **Duration:** 40 seconds per video (system default)
- **Hosting:** Any CDN or server

### Video Examples

You can use:
- ✅ Your own video files
- ✅ Cloudflare Stream videos
- ✅ AWS S3 videos
- ✅ Google Cloud Storage videos
- ✅ YouTube videos (if allowed)
